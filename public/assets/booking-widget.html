How Booking Works

Your appointment in 5 easy steps — takes about 2 minutes.

1
Choose Consultation Type
Pick online teleconsultation from home, or an in-clinic visit at Shri Hari Child Clinic.
2
Select a Doctor
Browse available paediatricians and choose the right specialist for your child.
3
Enter Patient Details
Fill in the child's name, date of birth, and reason for the visit.
4
Pick a Date & Time
Select from real-time available slots. Your chosen time is held for you.
5
Confirm & Pay
Review your booking summary, accept the terms, and confirm. Online appointments require payment via Cashfree. Clinic visits are pay-at-clinic.
✓
Receive Confirmation
You'll get an instant booking confirmation and a WhatsApp reminder ~30 minutes before your appointment.
